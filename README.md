One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout that hit
Asian markets (Kospi −3.3%), Guterres calling to regulate AI before it is
too late, and the US lead over China falling to a record low (gap 5.3).

**31 stories in one page:** a big number grid (10 numbers), 3 hero scenes,
10 cards, a world map with 13 numbered pins, an ALSO list of 18 more
stories, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps 22/21/19 November stories out.

Built for the [make.fun news-image-20261123 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).
`main.py` (stdlib only) verifies `entry.png` (1024×1024, ≤1 MiB) and writes
a byte-identical copy to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
